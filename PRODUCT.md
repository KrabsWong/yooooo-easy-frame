# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use Easy Frame on macOS to convert ordered PNG sequences into APNG images. They open settings occasionally to change presentation language, manage signed update checks, or inspect bundled component versions.

## Product Purpose

Easy Frame is a focused native macOS utility. Its settings should stay out of the workflow, use familiar macOS conventions, and make persistent choices obvious without ceremony.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, direct, native. The interface behaves like a well-behaved macOS utility rather than a branded marketing surface.

## Anti-references

Web-style dashboards, oversized modal dialogs, decorative cards, gradient accents, and custom controls that imitate but slightly differ from standard macOS form controls.

## Design Principles

Prefer native affordances over custom visuals.
Keep settings in a dedicated, predictable place.
Show state changes immediately without an extra save step.
Use compact grouping and restrained typography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labeled interactive controls, standard focus behavior, sufficient text contrast, and clear bilingual copy in English and Chinese.
